# Glossary (Module 11)

*Genomics with GoliathOmics · Goliath Research Academy* · [Module 11](11_content.md)

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| 510(k) | Premarket notification showing substantial equivalence to a predicate (21 CFR 807 Subpart E). Result: *cleared*. |
| Analytical validity | Whether a test measures the analyte accurately and reliably. |
| Audit trail | Secure, time-stamped record of who created, changed or deleted a record and when, without obscuring earlier values (21 CFR 11.10(e)). |
| CLIA | Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments; lab regulation in 42 CFR 493. |
| Clinical validity | Whether a test result is associated with the clinical condition. |
| De Novo | Classification request for a novel low-to-moderate-risk device without a predicate (21 CFR 860 Subpart D). |
| Design controls | Planned, reviewed, verified and validated design process with a design history (QMSR, ISO 13485 §7.3). |
| eSTAR | FDA's electronic submission template, required for 510(k)s since Oct 1, 2023 unless exempt. |
| Electronic signature | Signature linked to its record with signer, date/time and meaning (21 CFR 11 Subpart C). |
| Intended use | What the device is for and for whom; it drives classification and route. |
| IVD | In vitro diagnostic: a device that examines specimens taken from the body. |
| PMA | Premarket approval for Class III devices (21 CFR 814). Result: *approved*. |
| Predicate | A legally marketed device a 510(k) compares against. |
| Predicate rules | FDA record-keeping requirements outside Part 11 that make a record subject to Part 11 when kept electronically. |
| QMSR | Quality Management System Regulation, 21 CFR 820 as amended effective Feb 2, 2026, incorporating ISO 13485:2016. |
| SaMD | Software as a Medical Device: software that is itself the device. |
| Substantial equivalence | Same intended use and same or not-differently-risky technology, shown with performance data. |
